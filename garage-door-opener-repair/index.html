Common Opener Problems

Before assuming you need a new unit, many opener issues are a quick fix — misaligned sensors, worn gears, or a stripped trolley. We troubleshoot every brand and recommend repair when it saves you money.

  • Motor runs but door doesn't move
  • Remote or wall button not responding
  • Door reverses immediately after closing
  • Grinding, clicking, or buzzing sounds
  • Safety sensor alignment and wiring
  • Keypad and Wi-Fi / app connectivity
  • Full opener replacement and installation

Repair & Installation

We service LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, and other major brands. If repair isn't cost-effective, we'll quote a new belt-drive, chain-drive, or wall-mount jackshaft opener with battery backup and smart-home setup included.

Every opener job ends with a force and limit adjustment, safety reversal test, and remote programming. Serving Orange County seven days a week — Irvine, Tustin, Orange, Santa Ana, Anaheim, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, and the Inland Empire.
