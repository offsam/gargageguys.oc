Signs You Need a Spring Repair

Garage door springs carry the full weight of the door — hundreds of pounds of tension. When one breaks, the door feels heavy, won't open, or hangs crooked. Never try to replace springs yourself; call a professional.

  • Loud snap or bang from the garage
  • Door opens a few inches then stops
  • Visible gap in the torsion spring above the door
  • Door feels extremely heavy when lifting manually
  • Extension springs stretched or broken on side tracks
  • Opener strains, hums, or reverses without moving the door

Our Spring Service

We stock high-cycle torsion and extension springs for most residential doors. Our technician measures your door weight and height, matches the correct spring rating, and replaces both sides when needed so the door stays balanced.

Every spring job includes a balance check, lubrication, and hardware inspection. Parts carry a manufacturer warranty; our labor is warrantied up to one year.
