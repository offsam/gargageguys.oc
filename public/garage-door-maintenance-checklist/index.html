Garage Door Maintenance Checklist

A quiet door is a balanced door with clean tracks and fresh weather seal. Fifteen minutes twice a year prevents most of the emergency calls we run in July heat and January cold snaps.

Call Now (949) 539-0009

Twice a year, not every weekend

Spring and fall: open the door, pull the release, and lift by hand. It should stay halfway without slamming. If it drops, the springs are tired. See how long springs last.

Wipe the photo-eye lenses. Test auto-reverse with a 2x4 on the floor. The door should hit the board and go back up. If it tries to crush the board, stop using the opener until the force and sensors are set.

Lubricate metal rollers, hinges, and the torsion spring with a garage-door lube, not WD-40. Skip nylon rollers. Wipe old grease out of the tracks instead of packing more in. Replace the bottom rubber if daylight shows under the door. That seal keeps out water and rodents in older Orange County garages.

Look at the cables. A few broken strands means replace the cable, not tape it. Same-day hardware work: garage door repair in Orange County and the city list.

  • Balance test on the emergency release
  • Photo-eye wipe and 2x4 reverse test
  • Lube metal rollers and hinges only
  • Bottom seal and cable visual check
