What We Fix

From minor adjustments to full panel replacement, Garage Guys handles every type of garage door repair across Orange County — Irvine, Tustin, Orange, Santa Ana, Anaheim, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, and the Inland Empire.

  • Off-track or misaligned doors
  • Broken or frayed lift cables
  • Worn rollers, hinges, and brackets
  • Damaged panels and dent repair

  • Weather seal and bottom rubber replacement

How It Works

Call or request a callback — we'll confirm your address and give you a time window. Our technician arrives with common parts on the truck, inspects the full system (springs, cables, opener, tracks), and quotes the repair before any work starts. No hidden fees.

Most repairs are finished in under two hours. We stand behind our work with up to a one-year labor warranty and manufacturer parts warranties where applicable.
